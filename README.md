Vice Panels turns your photos into a multi-panel GTA-style poster cover, offline in your browser. Upload up to four photos, pick a layout (Hero, Duo, Trio, Quad, Mosaic or Diagonal), grade it with a Vice City sunset or neon wash, set a bold poster caption, and export a PNG in 1080 × 1080, 1080 × 1350 or 1920 × 1080. Every photo is cropped and positioned per panel — zoom, pan, or drag it right on the canvas — and everything is drawn by hand on canvas: no Rockstar logo, artwork or font anywhere, and no network request at all. Photos never leave your machine.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# open http://localhost:8080
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
